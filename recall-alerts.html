Know the moment your brand gets recalled — before your customers ask you about it

Free email alerts sourced straight from the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) and FDA public recall databases. Enter your email and your brand or product name — that's it.

How it works
1

Tell us what to watch

Your brand name, product line, or store name — whatever you'd search for if you were worried about a recall.

2

We check CPSC + FDA every day

Consumer products (toys, electronics, furniture, tools) via CPSC, and food/drug/medical device recalls via the FDA's openFDA database — one keyword, both agencies.

3

You get an email — only when there's something new

No daily digest noise. You only hear from us when a genuinely new recall matches, with a link to the official record.

Who this is for

Small sellers on Amazon, Shopify, Etsy, or your own store who sell (or resell) physical products, food, supplements, cosmetics, or devices — and want to know immediately if something in your supply chain gets recalled, without checking government websites by hand or hiring a compliance tool built for enterprises with a legal department.
