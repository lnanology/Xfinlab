5. Acceptable Use Policy — Prohibited Uses

Customer must not, and must not permit any End User or third party to:

  • Circumvent quota or rate limits, including by operating multiple accounts/keys to exceed a single Plan's allotted usage, or by any automated means designed to bypass the published limits.
  • Remove or obscure attribution required under Section 6.
  • Present API Content as individualized investment advice, a guarantee of future performance, or a licensed financial recommendation. All Content is informational/analytical output, not investment advice — see Section 9.
  • Use the API for any illegal purpose, including market manipulation, or in a manner that would cause XFINLAB to violate the terms of any of its own upstream data providers (see xfinlab.com's public data-source disclosures).

  • Use the Service to build a directly competing structured-intelligence or AI-news product primarily by re-serving XFINLAB's own Content rather than Customer's own added value.

Violation of this section may result in immediate suspension or termination of Customer's API Key(s) without refund of any prepaid fees, at XFINLAB's discretion, proportionate to the severity of the violation.
