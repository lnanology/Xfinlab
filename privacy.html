Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect email address, name, usage data, and (for anti-fraud and abuse-prevention purposes) device fingerprint signals, to provide, secure, and improve our services.

Your data is used to operate, secure, and improve the platform, and to let you save preferences such as watchlists and chat history. We never sell your personal data. Our legal basis for this processing is: contractual necessity (to provide the account and features you sign up for), legitimate interest (for security, fraud prevention, and service improvement), and consent (where you opt in, e.g. to push notifications or marketing emails).

3. Data Security

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard security practices.

4. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your data by contacting support@xfinlab.com

For all privacy inquiries, data requests, or to exercise any of the rights described on this page, contact support@xfinlab.com. 11. Hong Kong Data Protection (PDPO)

XFINLAB is the data user of your personal data under Hong Kong's Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486), and handles your data in accordance with the six Data Protection Principles under the Ordinance (covering the purpose and manner of collection, accuracy and retention, use, security, openness, and access and correction). You have the right to request access to, and correction of, your personal data; contact support@xfinlab.com to make such a request.

12. Automated Analysis

See our AI Disclaimer and Methodology pages for further detail.

Contact support@xfinlab.com if you would like more detail on the safeguards applicable to your data.

14. Right to Lodge a Complaint

If you are located in the EEA, UK, or another jurisdiction with a data protection supervisory authority, you have the right to lodge a complaint with that authority (for example, your national or regional data protection regulator) if you believe our processing of your personal data violates applicable law. We would appreciate the chance to address your concerns directly first — contact support@xfinlab.com.
