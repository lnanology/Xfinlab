Model Limitations

Last updated: August 2026

1. Data Lag & Gaps

Market data is sourced from third-party providers and may be delayed, incomplete, or briefly unavailable during provider outages. Analysis generated during a data gap may be based on stale or partial inputs.

2. Regime Change Risk

Our models, including probability and pattern-similarity estimates, are built on historical data. When market conditions shift meaningfully (for example, a change in interest-rate regime, a liquidity shock, or a structural change in an industry) historical patterns can stop predicting future behavior. A high historical similarity score does not mean the same outcome is likely to repeat.

3. Model & Data Coverage

Not every asset has equally deep historical data, news coverage, or liquidity history. Scores for thinly-traded or newly-listed assets are based on a smaller sample and carry wider uncertainty than scores for large, well-covered assets.

4. AI Model Limitations

Large language models used for narrative commentary and chat responses can misinterpret context, omit relevant information, or generate incorrect statements with high apparent confidence. We apply prompt design and monitoring to reduce this risk, but cannot eliminate it entirely.

5. Use Your Own Judgment

XFINLAB's tools are designed to inform, not replace, your own analysis and judgment. You should combine our research with your own due diligence and, where appropriate, professional advice before making any financial decision.
