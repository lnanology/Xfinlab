How This DCF Model Works

This is a standard 2-stage discounted cash flow model. Stage 1 projects free cash flow growing at your chosen rate for 5 years, then discounts each year back to today's dollars using your discount rate (WACC). Stage 2 estimates a terminal value -- the value of every cash flow after year 5 -- using the Gordon Growth formula (Year-5 cash flow × (1 + terminal growth) ÷ (discount rate − terminal growth)), also discounted back to today. Add the two stages together and divide by shares outstanding to get an estimated intrinsic value per share. DCF is extremely sensitive to its growth and discount rate assumptions -- small changes in either input can swing the result significantly, so treat this as one input among several, not a precise target price.
