Methodology

Last updated: August 2026

1. Research Score

The Research Score is produced by our Confluence Engine, which combines multiple real technical factors — trend direction, RSI, MACD, moving-average structure, support/resistance proximity, volume, and (where available) market-structure signals such as breaks of structure and liquidity sweeps — into a single weighted score. No single factor determines the output; the score reflects the aggregate weight of evidence across factors at the time of calculation.

2. Probability Estimates

Probability figures shown on XFINLAB (for example, "Historical Pattern Similarity") are derived from backtesting and historical statistical analysis of similar technical setups. They describe how often a comparable pattern was followed by a comparable outcome in historical data — they are not a prediction or guarantee of what will happen next. Markets are not stationary, and historical relationships can and do break down.

3. Risk Score

The Risk Score incorporates measures such as historical and implied volatility, drawdown behavior, and liquidity characteristics of the asset. A higher Risk Score indicates greater historical price variability or thinner liquidity, not a judgment about whether the asset is a "good" or "bad" investment.

4. News & Sentiment Score

Where shown, News and Sentiment Scores are derived from automated analysis of news articles and public discussion using natural-language processing models (including FinBERT-class sentiment classifiers), aggregated over a rolling time window. They reflect the tone of available public content, not a verified fact about the underlying company or asset.

5. Models, Not Guarantees

All scores and estimates on XFINLAB are the output of statistical and AI models applied to historical and current market data. They are research tools intended to help you form your own view — they are not guarantees of future performance and should not be treated as a substitute for your own judgment or professional advice. See our Model Limitations page for more detail.
