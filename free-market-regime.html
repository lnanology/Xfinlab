What Is Market Regime / VIX Term Structure?

The VIX term structure compares implied volatility across different time horizons (9-day, 30-day, 3-month, 6-month). When longer-dated volatility trades above near-term volatility, the curve is in contango -- the market's normal, calm state. When near-term volatility spikes above longer-dated volatility, the curve flips into backwardation -- historically a signal of acute market stress, since it means traders are paying up for protection right now more than they expect to need it later.
