How Position Sizing Works

Position sizing answers one question: how many shares can you buy so that if the stock hits your stop-loss, you only lose the amount you decided to risk -- not a random, uncomfortable number. The formula is straightforward: divide your dollar risk (account size × risk %) by your risk per share (the distance between your entry and your stop). Professional traders typically risk 0.5%-2% of their account on any single position, regardless of how confident they feel about the trade -- position sizing is what keeps one bad trade from doing serious account damage.
