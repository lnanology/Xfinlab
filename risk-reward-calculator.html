Why Risk/Reward Ratio Matters

A risk/reward (R:R) ratio compares how much you stand to lose against how much you stand to gain on a single trade. A 1:3 ratio means you're risking $1 to make $3 -- which matters because it directly sets the win rate you actually need to be profitable over time. At 1:1 you need to win more than 50% of trades just to break even (before costs); at 1:3 you only need to win more than 25%. This is why many traders focus on finding setups with a favorable R:R before they even think about win rate.
