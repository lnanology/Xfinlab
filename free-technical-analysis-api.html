What Is a Confluence Signal?

A confluence signal combines several independent technical readings (trend, RSI, MACD, volume, and more) into one weighted score, rather than trusting any single indicator on its own -- the more signals that agree, the higher the confidence. This preview shows the top-level direction, confidence, trend and RSI; the full API also returns MACD, market structure (break of structure, change of character, liquidity sweeps), chart pattern detection and decision levels.
