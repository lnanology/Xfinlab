IEX Cloud Alternative

IEX Group shut down IEX Cloud entirely on August 31, 2024, giving developers roughly three months' notice and no successor product. If your app's data layer still needs replacing, here's a straight comparison -- no exaggerated claims, sources linked below.

What Actually Happened

IEX Cloud's shutdown was announced in March 2024 and took effect August 31, 2024. IEX Group's stated reason was that the product ran at a loss and made up under 2% of IEX Group's overall revenue. Affected developers were pointed toward third-party migration options such as Intrinio, which multiple reviewers flagged as considerably more expensive for comparable fundamentals coverage (cited around $9,600/year in some breakdowns). Alpha Vantage and Finage were also commonly cited as where refugee developers migrated.

This section summarizes third-party reporting on the shutdown, not an XFINLAB claim about IEX Group. See sources at the bottom of this page.
