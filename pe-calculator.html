What Is the P/E Ratio?

The price-to-earnings (P/E) ratio divides a stock's current price by its earnings per share (EPS). It shows how much investors are paying today for each dollar of a company's annual earnings -- a higher P/E generally means the market expects faster future growth (or the stock is simply more expensive relative to its current profits), while a lower P/E can signal either a bargain or genuine risk the market is pricing in. P/E is most useful when compared against a company's own history and its direct sector peers, not as a standalone number.
